We check each tool you name and tell you what we found. Most connect when you sign in and say yes on the call. Some take a little more work. Common questions

I'm not technical. Who sets it up?

We do, with you. On a short call you sign in and approve access. We connect it, test it with you and hand it over.

Is setup included, and do my tools cost extra?

In the pilot, setup is included. After that it is part of Quick start, which comes with Business. See Pricing. You keep paying for your own tools directly. Some only allow a connection on certain plans, for example Payday Premium or higher.

My tool is not on the list. Can you connect it?

Probably. Tell us the name. If it has an export or a way in, we can usually connect it. If we can't, we say so and offer another way.

Do I have to leave my accounting system?

No. X sends the invoices there, and your bookkeeper keeps working as before. Where e-invoicing is required by law, your accounting system or e-invoice provider still issues the invoice. X prepares it.
