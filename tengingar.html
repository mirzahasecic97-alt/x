    How connecting works

    You connect it yourself. You stay in control.

    No code and no technician. Every connection comes with step-by-step guides.

    01

    Sign in

    For Google and Microsoft you click Connect and sign in with them. X never gets your password.

    02

    Or paste a key

    For DK, Payday and WooCommerce you create a key there and paste it in. The guide shows you where.

    03

    A green dot

    When the connection works, a green dot lights up. If it breaks, you get an email and see what to fix.

    Encrypted keys

    Keys are stored encrypted and only used on the server. They are never shown again.

    Never shown to the AI

    Claude and ChatGPT work with the data, never with the keys.

    Always asks first

    Anything the AI does in another system waits for your approval and is logged.

    Disconnect any time

    One click and the connection is cut. The keys are deleted.

    Only what is needed

    A ready template for each tool. The connection only does what the template allows.

    Database in Europe

    Every company is kept separate. The AI runs with Anthropic or OpenAI.

    Questions

    About connections

    Do I have to leave Payday or DK?

    No. X sends the invoices there. Your bookkeeper keeps working as before.

    What if my tool is not on the list?

    If it issues API keys, it can come as a ready template. Until then, Zapier and Make connect the rest. Tell us what you use.

    What happens if a connection breaks?

    The dot turns red and you get an email about what to fix. The rest of the system keeps working.

    I have a Shopify store. Does it work?

    It's coming soon. You create an app in your store and paste the keys into X. We help you.

    Are Repeat and Torfi connected?

    Not yet. The connections are coming and will be marked live when they work. The Torfi connection comes when Torfi opens an API. Until then, invoices go to Torfi as a file.

    Can I use my Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus subscription?

    Not as a key in the system. AI is included in X and you can connect your own key. A connector inside Claude and ChatGPT is coming.

    I make software. Can I connect to X?

    Yes. Read about building a connection, or write to us and we'll send you the description.
