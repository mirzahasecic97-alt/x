Your data

Straight talk about your data.

Where your data is stored, and where it goes when the AI works.

Database in Europe

Your company's data is stored in a database in Europe.

The AI runs elsewhere

When the AI works, the data it needs goes to Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT). That processing is not limited to Europe.

Training

Under Anthropic's and OpenAI's terms, data sent through their APIs is not used to train their models by default. Read their terms: Anthropic, OpenAI.

Only your company

The AI only sees the data of the company it works for. Only your own people see your data.

Keys encrypted

Keys are stored encrypted and the AI never sees them.

Disconnect in one click

Each connection disconnects in one click and its keys are deleted.

Questions

About the AI

Can the AI send or pay for anything on its own?

No. It prepares and you approve. It never sends, pays or deletes anything without your approval, and everything is logged.

Can I use my Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus subscription?

Not as a key in X, because that kind of subscription is not an API key. AI is included in X and you can connect your own API key. X inside Claude and ChatGPT is coming soon.

Claude or ChatGPT, which should I pick?

Both work in X. If you already use one, it makes sense to start there. You can switch in settings.

What happens if I use up the AI for the month?

You see your use on the meter and you'll be told. Then you can wait for next month, move to a bigger plan or connect your own key. The rest of the system keeps working as before.

Where is my data?

The database is in Europe. When the AI works, the data it needs goes to Anthropic or OpenAI, depending on whether you pick Claude or ChatGPT.

Do I need to know about AI?

No. The morning list and the drafts just show up. If you want to learn more, the guides are in plain language.
