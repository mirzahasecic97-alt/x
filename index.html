What makes X different

Systems for contractors already exist. Most of them ask your crew to install a new app and learn it. X is different in three ways.

No new app.

X works in the chat your crew already has on their phones. Nothing to install, nothing to learn.

Every language.

Everyone writes and talks in their own language. X translates, both ways.

It asks before the crew goes.

X asks for what is missing before a worker leaves, and learns from every finished job in your firm.

Common questions

Which chat apps does X work in?

We are starting with WhatsApp and Messenger. Each person has their own chat with X, one to one. X does not sit in your group chat.

How good are the time estimates?

Rough at the start, and we say so. X's first job is to ask the right questions before a worker leaves. It learns from every finished job in your firm, so the estimates get closer over time.

Does my crew need to install anything?

No. They use the chat they already have. They write, send voice notes and photos like they do today.

Who pays?

The firm. Your workers never pay anything.

What does it cost?

The pilot is free. We set the price together with the pilot firms, before anyone pays anything.

Does X track where my crew is?

No. X only knows what people send in the chat.

Can X send invoices or connect to my accounting?

Not in the first version. You get hours and materials per job as Excel or PDF, ready for the system you invoice from today.
