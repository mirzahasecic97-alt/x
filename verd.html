Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Pilot firms use X free for 3 months. When we open, every plan starts with 14 days free.

Does the price go up when I hire?

Not within your plan. Team covers up to 5 people and Business up to 20, at the same price. If you grow past that, you move up a plan and everything comes with you.

Who connects my tools, and do they cost extra?

We do, with you. Your accounting system stays where it is. You keep paying for your own tools directly. Some only allow a connection on certain plans, for example Payday Premium or higher. We tell you before we start.

How do the work diary and hours work?

Your crew use your own app on their phones, with your name and logo. Not Messenger or WhatsApp. You decide who gets which jobs, and each worker sees only his own. He taps Started and Done and adds photos, hours and materials. The work diary fills in, and you approve the hours. X does not track where your workers are. Coming soon on Team and Business. If you want, a text or WhatsApp message can tell a worker a new task is waiting. Those are charged per message and may be added at cost. We will confirm this before launch.

Do you handle service contracts?

Yes, for contractors who work for companies on service agreements. You plan the day once in your own system and X turns it into tasks for each worker, most urgent first. Each job gets a short brief, and X flags a job that runs over. No extra fee. See the example.

Can tasks be translated for my crew?

Yes, on request.
