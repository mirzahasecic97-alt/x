Questions

Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Free for pilot firms. 14-day free trial when we open. Pilot firms use X free for 3 months. For everyone else, every plan starts with 14 days free before the first charge goes on your card.

Is there a setup fee?

No. You can start on your own. Setup with us is optional and paid once, from €290 for a quick start. On Business the quick start is included. There are no add-on fees either: no price per module or per user.

How do I pay?

By card, monthly with no commitment, or yearly: pay for 10 months and get 12, so 2 months free. On Business you can also pay by invoice with a 12-month agreement. Prices are excl. VAT, and your local VAT is added. If a payment doesn't go through, you're told and can update your card.

What does each plan cost?

Solo is €49, Team €129 and Business €299 a month, excl. VAT. Yearly it is €490, €1,290 and €2,990.

Does the price go up when I hire?

Not within your plan's size. Team covers up to 5 people and Business up to 20, at the same price. When the team grows past that, you move to a bigger plan, and your data and settings come along.

We are more than 20. What then?

We'll work it out together. Book a 15 min call.

Who sets up the connections?

We do, with you. You tell us what you use, we connect it and test it with you. Setup of connections is included in the pilot and in Quick start. How we connect your tools.

Do the connections cost extra?

You keep paying for the systems you connect, directly to them. Some charge separately for access. For example, the Payday connection needs Payday Premium or higher.

How does crew chat work?

You write or speak a task on WhatsApp in your language. Each worker gets it in their own language and answers the same way, by text, voice note or photo, and you read the answer in yours. Nothing goes out until you tap Send. X files the task, the replies, the photos and the start and finish times under the job, so the work diary and the hours fill in on their own, and you approve the hours. WhatsApp needs Meta to approve X first. Until then, and for workers without WhatsApp, a text message with a private link opens the task in their language, with no app and no login. X does not track where your workers are. Crew chat is coming soon on Team and Business. WhatsApp charges per message, and those fees may be added at cost. See the example.

Do I have to leave my accounting system?

No. X sends the invoices there. Your bookkeeper keeps working as before.

How soon can I start?

You can join the pilot now. The first release opens in [Month], and pilot firms start then. On your first day you pick your industry, add your price list and send your first quote.

I have a Shopify store. Does it work?

It's coming soon. You approve access in your store, and we connect it and test it with you.

What happens if I use up the AI for the month?

You'll be told, and the AI pauses until next month. Everything else in the system keeps working as before. If you need more, move to a bigger plan or connect your own Claude or OpenAI key.

Am I locked in?

No. Monthly plans have no commitment and you can cancel any time. Yearly plans run for the year you paid. Either way, you take all your data with you if you leave.

Do I need to be good with computers or code?

No. You pick your industry and we set up your connections with you. The guides walk you through the rest. If anything gets stuck, you get an answer from us.

I make software. Can I connect to X?

Yes. Read about building a connection for software companies.
