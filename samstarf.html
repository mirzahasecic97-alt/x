Questions

Questions and answers.

Does the description cost anything?

No. The description is open and free for anyone to implement. Anything else, such as referrals, we agree on in the contract.

Can we still work with others?

Yes. No exclusivity, either way.

Who owns the customer relationship?

Each company bills for its own product and keeps its own relationship with the customer. The customer connects it themselves and disconnects in one click.

Can the AI change data in our system?

Not without approval. The MCP server is read-only. Every write goes through the actions in the description and the user's approval in X, and everything is logged.

Where do the data and keys live?

X's database is in Europe. Keys are stored encrypted and the AI never sees them. The manifest states where your data lives.

What if our API changes?

You tell us ahead of time and we update the connection. The tests keep running. A connection that stops working comes off the list until it is fixed.
