The flow

From enquiry to payment. Who does what.

Seven steps, one line through the system. Nothing is typed twice and nothing goes out without your yes.

  1. 01

    An enquiry comes in

    Web form, Outlook, forwarding from Gmail

    X

    The enquiry lands in Sales and the AI drafts a reply.

    You

    Read the reply and send it.

  2. 02

    A quote from your price list

    X

    Builds the quote in one version or three. It can start from a description or a photo.

    You

    Adjust what's needed and send the link.

  3. 03

    Your customer chooses

    Your customer

    Opens the link, picks a version and accepts online.

    X

    Tells you whether the quote was opened and what was chosen.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    X

    Prepares a reminder after 3 and 7 days if there's no reply.

    You

    Approve it on the morning list with one tap.

  5. 05

    Becomes a job

    X

    The accepted quote becomes a job and its lines become tasks.

    You

    Hand the tasks to your people.

  6. 06

    Invoice to your accounting

    DK, Payday

    More local systems for each country we open in.

    X

    Drafts the invoice from the quote or the job, in one go or in stages.

    You

    Approve it and the invoice goes to your accounting system.

  7. 07

    Payment

    Straumur, Teya, Pei

    Coming soon
    X

    Creates a payment link on your own agreement and sees whether it was paid.

    Your customer

    Pays by card or with Pei.

X never holds your money. Payments go through your own agreement with your card acquirer, and your books stay where they are.

Crew tasks and work diary Coming soon

Each worker gets his tasks on his phone, by a text message link or on WhatsApp. He taps Started and Done and adds photos or a voice note. X keeps the work diary per job and the hours per worker, and you approve them. Nothing goes out until you tap Send.

  • Tasks to each worker, by link or WhatsApp
  • Started, Done and photos from the job
  • A work diary per job and hours per worker
  • You approve the hours before they go anywhere

A text message link needs no app and no login. WhatsApp comes once Meta approves X. The work diary helps you keep the official site diary. It does not replace it. Crew speaks another language? We can turn on translation for your tasks.

Service contracts

For plumbers, electricians and maintenance crews who work for companies on service agreements. The day's list comes in once, and every job is checked before anyone calls it a quick one.

See the example
  • The day's list, sent once. Paste or type it, or send it to your jobs address. Each worker gets his own tasks, most urgent first. On his phone with crew tasks Coming soon
  • A job brief first. The client's photos, past visits to the same address, a short checklist and an estimate.
  • Planned against actual. A job that runs over is flagged, and a short note to the client waits for your OK.
  • Materials on the job. The supplier's invoice is matched to the job by the job number given at the counter. Coming soon
  • One board for status. Started, waiting for parts, or done with a photo.
