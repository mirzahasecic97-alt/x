Guides / Start here

Your first day in X

  1. Your company

    Name, company ID, logo and main colour. Everything you send out carries your look.

    In the system: Settings › Company

  2. Your industry

    Contractors (trades and renovation), Cleaning and services, Consultants or Shops. The right modules switch on, and you can change them later.

    In the system: Settings › Modules

  3. Your team

    Invite colleagues by email. If you work alone, skip this step.

    In the system: Settings › Team

  4. Your connections

    Gmail or Outlook, your calendar and your accounting: DK or Payday with a key. Claude or ChatGPT if you like.

    In the system: Connections

  5. Your data in

    Customers and your price list from Excel or CSV, or straight from your accounting system.

    In the system: Customers › Import

The goal: your first quote goes out the same day.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I use X now?

Not yet. The first release opens in [Month]. Pilot: the first 10 firms use X free for 3 months. In return: a short call every two weeks and honest feedback. After the pilot: 25% off for the first 12 months.

Are the videos out?

Not yet. Every guide starts as an article with screenshots, and the video follows.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Claude 101 and ChatGPT 101 start at the very beginning, in plain language. You learn by doing, on your own work.

What language are the guides in?

English for now. X is built for local markets, starting in Europe, and the guides follow each market we open in.

What if I get stuck?

Inside the system there is a button that sends us a message. You get an answer from a person. During the pilot you also have a short call with us every two weeks.
